## Selection rationale

- **Nuvole Bianche:** Prefer San over SarVxV (longer, 6.4 s rest) and the thinner Kalimba arrangement.
- **Interstellar Main Theme:** Prefer balanced density and repeat margin over the Peter Buka MIDI-derived arrangement (34 ms repeat); other versions were shorter.
- **Merry Christmas Mr. Lawrence:** Prefer fuller chord texture over Sunny; winter piano version had a 20 ms repeat and 21 s rest; Ustinian is a short excerpt.
- **Comptine d'un autre été:** Prefer fuller arrangement over Cova, Cractycusly and the sparse recorded candidate; melodic fidelity still needs listening.
- **The Truth That You Leave:** Prefer 213 s chord-rich recording over the 44 s candidate.
- **Mariage d'Amour:** Prefer longer one-Piano arrangement over the 68 s ikina candidate.
- **Viva La Vida:** Strong rhythmic chord texture; other named variants were not exhaustively compared.
- **Hallelujah:** Prefer this long chorded version over the 38 s Haven recording; review four-track merge and seven-key chords.
- **Kamado Tanjiro no Uta:** Balanced candidate; alternate arrangements not exhaustively evaluated.
- **Sweden:** Chorded single-Piano candidate; alternate Sweden arrangements not exhaustively evaluated.

### Second batch: selection rationale

- **A Cruel Angel's Thesis:** DonniQ is a chorded, credited short arrangement; full Japanese-titled candidate has a 13 ms same-key interval and fails the independent default 60 FPS hold screen. Compare against hard version and assess completeness.
- **What Was I Made For:** Solar uses two piano tracks, fuller chord texture than the other screened piano version; Lei uses a guitar track with distinct pitch.
- **Young and Beautiful:** 语瞳 is a single-piano arrangement with more chord onsets and shorter maximum rest than the other screened longer version.
- **Nothing's New:** Solar is substantially longer than the 10-second excerpt; upstream artist spelling is Reo Romeo, display title uses Rio Romeo. External Cryomgi candidate was not decoded.
- **Suzume:** Jyun balances duration, texture and repeat spacing; Full Main Theme remains an alternate with denser chords, more events and shorter repeat spacing.
- **From the Start:** Kiki has one track and 15-key coverage; compare rhythmic fidelity against Nugget and Solar.
- **Past Lives:** 语瞳 has more chorded notes than the short Sapientdream candidates; upstream artist field is Slushii. Verify arrangement identity by listening.
- **Heather:** LVCATIE has fuller chord texture; compare with the longer one-piano and Thiago versions for completeness.
- **Rewrite the Stars:** Jyun has longer duration and fuller texture than the screened alternatives.
- **deja vu:** Select the explicitly Olivia Rodrigo-titled all-piano version; inspect four-track merge and seven-key chords.
- **Goodbye to a World:** Solar has a longer chorded arrangement than 空 and monophonic alternatives.
- **Snowman:** Emman uses all 15 keys and has clear arrangement credit; compare against recorded v2.
- **Talking to the Moon:** One-piano version has fuller chord texture; verify tempo and repeated sections because of its longer duration.
- **Count on Me:** Yun云 uses one track and has more generous repeat spacing than Solar.
- **One Call Away:** Solar has fuller chord texture, fewer tracks and explicit credit.

### Deliberately not added

- **Yume to Hazakura:** Existing built-in retained. The AkiCutieGamingMusic composed v3 candidate matched all 736 onset key sets, 1,368 events and active duration in the independent comparison; no demonstrated musical improvement warrants replacing the existing stable entry.
- **Speechless:** Generic source title and missing artist attribution do not establish that the arrangement is Naomi Scott's Aladdin song. No verified alternative obtained; do not ship an uncertain match.
