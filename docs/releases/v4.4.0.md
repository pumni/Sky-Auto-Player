# Sky Auto Player v4.4.0

## Improved
- Simplified the Player Dock with clearer playback status and Test mode indicators.
- Improved built-in song browsing with Locate Selected, compact pagination, and long-title tooltips.
- Updated playback scheduling and key-release timing for repeated notes and chords.

## Fixed
- Restored two previously unplayable built-in songs: *Only (LeeHi)* and *Stay With Me (Goblin)*.
- Prevented timing advisory pop-ups from briefly appearing when playback starts.
