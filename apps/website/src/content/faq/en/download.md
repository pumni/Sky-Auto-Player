question: 'Where can I download Sky Auto Player?'
---

Download the canonical Tauri NSIS installer from the [official Sky Auto Player GitHub Releases page](https://github.com/pumni/Sky-Auto-Player/releases). It installs for the current user. The `.exe.sig` file is used by the in-app updater; it is not needed for a manual install. Windows may show an Unknown Publisher or SmartScreen warning because the installer is not Authenticode-signed under the current policy.
