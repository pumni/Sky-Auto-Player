question: 'Does Sky Auto Player need admin rights?'
---

The portable app does not require an installer or administrator access for normal use. To remove it, close the app and delete its folder after preserving any songs or configuration you want to keep. **Update now** writes the staged transaction to the existing portable folder; close other programs that have files in that folder if Windows reports a lock.
